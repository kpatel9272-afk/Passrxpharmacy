What the notes are

PassRx revision notes are concise PDFs written by a practising pharmacist. Each topic summarises the essentials for exam revision and is designed to be used alongside current NICE guidance and the BNF. Notes are updated when guidance changes, and each is a PDF download. How to use the notes

  1. Read a topic once, then close it and write what you remember.
  2. Do practice questions on the topic the next day.
  3. Note the gaps in your mistakes log and re-read only those sections.
  4. Revisit each topic at least twice before your exam.
