GPhC Registration Assessment 2027: What You Need to Know

A summary of the 2027 sitting based on GPhC information, a preparation timeline and the resources to use. Dates are in one box so they are easy to update.

Last updated: 4 November 2026 · Written by a practising pharmacist

Always confirm with the GPhC. The facts below are summarised from GPhC publications at the date shown and may change. This page is not official GPhC information. Check the GPhC website before booking or making plans.

Key facts (checked 4 November 2026)

  • 2026 sitting: 10 November 2026.
  • 2027 sitting: 15 June 2027.
  • Results for the June 2027 sitting: 20 July 2027.
  • Part 1 (calculations): 40 questions in 2 hours.
  • Part 2: 120 questions in 2 hours 30 minutes.
  • Attempts: a maximum of three.
  • Booking, eligibility, fees and permitted items: see the GPhC. We do not summarise these because they change.

What candidates need to know

The assessment is taken by pre-registration trainee pharmacists as part of qualifying to register. It tests your ability to apply knowledge to practice. Read the GPhC's current specification and regulations for the sitting you intend to take, including rules on booking, adjustments, permitted items and conduct.
