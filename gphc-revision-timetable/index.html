GPhC Revision Timetable: 12, 8 and 4-Week Plans

Choose the plan that fits the time you have left, then adapt it to your weak areas and working pattern.

Last updated: 4 November 2026 · Written by a practising pharmacist

Official information first. Dates, format, permitted items and eligibility are set by the GPhC and can change. Use the GPhC registration assessment pages as your source of truth; see also our 2027 exam hub.

Before you start

Check your sitting date on the GPhC site, work back from it, and block out work, rest and holidays first. Revise little and often. Use the dates below as an example only: they count back from a Tuesday 15 June 2027 sitting, which you should confirm at the GPhC.

PlanStart date (example)Best for
12 weeks23 March 2027Steady revision alongside full-time work
8 weeks20 April 2027A good base already
4 weeks18 May 2027Consolidation only, if you started late
Final week8 June 2027Everyone

12-week approach

  • Weeks 1–2: Read the assessment specification, rate your confidence by topic, set up a mistakes log. Start daily calculations.
  • Weeks 3–8: Two or three clinical topics a week, with law and ethics once a week. Do questions the day after each topic.
  • Weeks 9–10: Revisit weak topics. First timed mock.
  • Weeks 11–12: Second mock, review, light consolidation.

8-week approach

  • Weeks 1–5: Weakest topics first, around four a week, plus daily calculations.
  • Weeks 6–7: Mixed question sets and one timed mock.
  • Week 8: Review and rest.

4-week approach

  • Week 1: Calculations and law, the most learnable marks.
  • Weeks 2–3: High-yield clinical topics through questions, not long reading.
  • Week 4: One timed mock, review and consolidate.

The final week

  • Review your mistakes log rather than starting new topics.
  • Do short calculation sets daily.
  • Check the exam logistics and permitted items on the GPhC site.
  • Sleep, eat and take a proper break the day before.

Practice-question strategy

Aim for a daily set of mixed questions, review every explanation, and revisit wrong answers after one day, one week and one month. Add a timed mock every two to three weeks. See our mock exam guide for how to review.

Fill your weeks with the right material

Use the revision notes for each topic and practise with a timed mock as your sitting gets closer.
